Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

WEBAURA by iii respects your privacy. This page explains what information may be collected when you use this website or submit a project inquiry.

Information you provide

If you submit the inquiry form, you may provide your name, business name, email address, phone number, requested service, approximate budget and project description.

How information is used

Information is used to review and respond to project inquiries, understand the requested work and communicate about a potential engagement. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information through this form.

Storage and service providers

When configured, inquiry data may be stored using Google Firebase/Cloud Firestore. The website is designed to be hosted on Vercel. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy documentation. Firebase must be configured with restrictive security rules before live use.

Retention and sharing

Inquiry information should be retained only as long as needed to handle the inquiry and meet applicable legal obligations. It is not intended to be sold. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the website or disclosed where legally required.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of information you submitted, subject to applicable law. Contact details for privacy requests must be added by the website owner before launch.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not intentionally include an analytics tracker in this starter version. Third-party assets such as hosted fonts and Firebase may process technical information when loaded. Add a notice if analytics, advertising or additional tracking is introduced.

Children

This website is intended for business inquiries and is not directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the website and services change. The website owner should review it for compliance with applicable law before launch.
